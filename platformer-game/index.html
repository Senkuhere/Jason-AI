Combat Manual & Controls

Master your weapon and abilities to survive the monster onslaught.

Agile Movement

Use Left/Right arrow keys or A/D to maneuver across platforms. Timing your jumps is crucial to avoid falling or monster ambushes.

Melee Slash Combo

Press J or Z to swing your enchanted blade. Consecutive hits deal devastating combo damage to beasts.

Shadow Dash

Press K or X to dash through enemies instantly, avoiding damage while cutting down anything in your path.
